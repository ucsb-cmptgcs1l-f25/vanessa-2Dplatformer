# 2D Platform Adventure
Sproutcast is set in various biomes where our Sprout must strategically build to reach the other side. The Sprout must retrieve different resources from the environment, only able to carry one thing at a time. Each resource in the environment has different pros and cons. For instance, the Sprout starts in the forest biome. Each biome has three levels, which rise in difficulty. On the levels, the Sprout must reach the other side of the bridge to complete the level. In this example, our Sprout can pick up twigs, which are light but break easily in the bridge. Otherwise, they can grab a wooden log, which is heavy (slows down Sprout) but is sturdier in the bridge. The player uses their creativity to figure out the best way to reach the other side. After completing the level and moving on to the next, the player may have more time pressure or have a more difficult biome, such as more wind or progress into the snow biome.
